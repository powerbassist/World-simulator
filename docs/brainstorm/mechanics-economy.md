# Mechanics — Economy

Unfiltered candidate list. The tier column is a first-pass read; [[mvp]], [[v1]], [[someday]] and [[out-of-scope-mvp]] are the authority.

## Public finance

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Spending lines | Health, education, defence, pensions, infrastructure, debt service. Each an allocation the player sets. | MVP |
| Income tax | Rate the player controls, with a diminishing-returns curve and a political cost. | MVP |
| Corporate tax | Same, plus a drag on investment. | MVP |
| VAT / consumption tax | Regressive, quiet, effective. | MVP |
| Deficit and national debt | Spending beyond revenue accumulates; debt service crowds out everything else. | MVP |
| Bond yields | The cost of borrowing rises as debt and instability rise. | V1 |
| Credit rating | An external judgement that gates borrowing cost in visible steps. | V1 |
| Progressive tax brackets | Distributional control rather than a single rate. | V1 |
| Wealth and capital gains taxes | High symbolic value, modest revenue, real capital flight risk. | V1 |
| Tariffs and customs duties | Revenue plus a foreign policy lever. | V1 |
| Tax evasion and enforcement | Spend on collection to close the gap between nominal and actual take. | Someday |
| Austerity and stimulus packages | Named, temporary, high-drama fiscal programmes. | Someday |
| Sovereign wealth fund | Bank the surplus, if there ever is one. | Someday |
| Default | The end of a debt spiral, and a very loud lose condition. | Someday |

## Real economy

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Aggregate sectors | Agriculture, industry, services, energy — each with output, employment share, and health. | MVP |
| GDP and growth | Derived from sector health, investment, and shocks. | MVP |
| Unemployment | The number that drives public mood harder than any other. | MVP |
| Inflation | Prices as a separate pressure from growth. | V1 |
| Business cycle | Booms and recessions that arrive whether or not the player deserves them. | V1 |
| Sectoral investment | Direct state money at a sector and wait several years. | V1 |
| Infrastructure projects | Discrete, expensive, slow, visible on the map. | V1 |
| Energy mix | Nuclear, renewables, fossil, imports — a policy fight with an emissions and dependency consequence. | Someday |
| Housing market | Its own crisis generator. | Someday |
| Automation and productivity | Long-horizon growth versus employment tension. | Someday |
| Regional economic divergence | Prosperity concentrating, and the politics that follow. | Someday |

## Private companies

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Named companies per nation | Fictional firms per [[fictionalization]], each in a sector with revenue and headcount. | V1 |
| Subsidies | Pay a company to stay, hire, or invest. | V1 |
| Sector regulation | Rules that constrain firms and cost them margin. | V1 |
| Nationalisation | Take a company into state hands. Expensive, popular with some, ruinous with investors. | V1 |
| Privatisation | Sell state assets for a one-off windfall and a permanent loss of control. | V1 |
| Corporate lobbying | Firms spend to shift policy and party positions. | Someday |
| Autonomous corporate decisions | Firms independently invest, relocate, hire, and fire based on the policy environment. | Someday |
| Foreign direct investment | Other nations' companies entering yours, and vice versa. | Someday |
| Strategic asset protection | Blocking a foreign takeover of something politically sensitive. | Someday |
| Bankruptcies and bailouts | A large employer fails and the player chooses. | Someday |
| State-owned enterprises | Firms the state already runs, with their own profit and patronage dynamics. | Someday |

## Monetary policy

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Central bank | Sets interest rates, with an independence setting the player can attack. | Someday |
| Interest rates | The blunt instrument for inflation and growth. | Someday |
| Currency and exchange rate | Only meaningful for nations with their own currency — which excludes most of the MVP roster. | Someday |
| Monetary union constraints | Eurozone membership meaning the rate is set elsewhere. Realistic and quietly the reason monetary policy is not an MVP concern for France. | Someday |
| Quantitative easing | Crisis-era money creation, with an inflation bill later. | Someday |
| Capital controls | Stop money leaving. Drastic. | Someday |
