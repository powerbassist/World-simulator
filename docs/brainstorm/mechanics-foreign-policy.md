# Mechanics — Foreign Policy

Unfiltered candidate list. The tier column is a first-pass read; [[mvp]], [[v1]], [[someday]] and [[out-of-scope-mvp]] are the authority.

> [!note] Nothing here is in the MVP
> Neighbouring nations exist at MVP as data and economic context — trade volumes that affect your growth, a map you can look at. They are not negotiation partners until [[v1]]. This is the single largest deliberate cut in the project; the reasoning is in [[out-of-scope-mvp]].

## Bilateral relations

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Relations score | A per-pair number driving what each nation will accept from the other. | V1 |
| Diplomatic actions | State visits, condemnations, recognition, expulsions — discrete moves that shift relations. | V1 |
| Embassies | Presence that improves information and relations over time. | V1 |
| Foreign aid | Money for influence. | V1 |
| Cultural and educational exchange | Slow, cheap, durable soft power. | Someday |
| Diaspora politics | Populations abroad as a lever and a liability. | Someday |

## Trade

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Trade volume between nations | Aggregate flows feeding into each nation's growth. Present at MVP as a **read-only** input. | MVP (passive) |
| Bilateral trade agreements | Negotiated deals lowering barriers, with sector winners and losers at home. | V1 |
| Tariffs as a weapon | Raise costs on a specific partner and absorb the retaliation. | V1 |
| Export controls | Deny a specific good to a specific nation. | Someday |
| Resource dependency | Energy and materials you cannot produce, and who holds them. | Someday |
| Supply chain disruption | External shocks propagating through trade links. | Someday |

## Blocs and alliances

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Supranational membership | EU-style bloc with shared rules, transfers, and constraints on domestic policy. | V1 |
| Bloc-level voting | Your vote in a body where you are one member among many. | V1 |
| Defensive alliances | Mutual commitments that pull you into other people's problems. | V1 |
| Accession and withdrawal | Joining or leaving a bloc — a multi-year domestic fight in its own right. | Someday |
| International organisations | UN-shaped bodies, vetoes, and resolutions. | Someday |
| Treaty ratification | Signed abroad, then fought over in your own assembly. | Someday |

## Coercion and conflict

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Sanctions | Economic pressure, imposed and received, with domestic blowback. | V1 |
| Military strength | An abstract capability score rather than units on a map. Derived from sustained defence spending, not authored. | V1 |
| War declaration and resolution | Resolved at the strategic level — costs, casualties, outcomes — not tactically. | Someday |
| Military deployment abroad | Peacekeeping and intervention, with a domestic approval bill. The bill *is* the mechanic. | V1 |
| Arms sales | Revenue, influence, and later regret. | Someday |
| Espionage | Intelligence gathering, sabotage, covert influence on other nations' politics. | Someday |
| Counter-intelligence | Catching theirs. | Someday |
| Cyber operations | The modern version, cheaper and deniable. | Someday |
| Border disputes | Contested territory as a persistent relations drag. | Someday |
| Nuclear deterrent | Possession, posture, and the politics of both. | Someday |

## Non-state and global

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| International markets reacting to you | Bond markets and investors as an external judge of domestic policy. | V1 |
| Migration flows between nations | Driven by economic and political conditions on both ends. | Someday |
| Climate accords | Binding emissions commitments with domestic economic cost. | Someday |
| Pandemics and global crises | Externally generated shocks hitting every nation at once. | Someday |
| Terrorism | Events with outsized political consequence relative to material damage. | Someday |
