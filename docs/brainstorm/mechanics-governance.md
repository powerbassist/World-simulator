# Mechanics — Governance

Unfiltered candidate list. Nothing here is a commitment; the tier column is a first-pass read, and [[mvp]], [[v1]], [[someday]] and [[out-of-scope-mvp]] are the authority.

## Elections and mandate

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-term general elections | Assembly seats contested on a set cycle. The clock the whole game runs on. | MVP |
| Presidential/executive election | Separate contest for the head of state where the system has one. | MVP |
| Polling | Rolling approval and voting-intention numbers, with noise so they can't be read as ground truth. | MVP |
| Campaign phase | A window before the vote where the player spends time and money on messaging. | V1 |
| Campaign promises | Pledges that bind: breaking them costs trust with specific voter blocs. | V1 |
| Electoral system choice | First-past-the-post vs proportional vs two-round, each producing different assemblies. | V1 |
| Gerrymandering / boundary review | Redraw districts. Powerful, slow, and enormously unpopular if noticed. | Someday |
| By-elections | Individual seats fall vacant and are recontested mid-term. | Someday |
| Turnout modelling | Apathy as a distinct force from opposition. | Someday |
| Voting age and franchise changes | Extend or restrict who votes. | Someday |

## Legislature

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Bill proposal and vote | Player drafts a law, assembly votes, effects apply on passage. The core verb. | MVP |
| Party seat counts | Assembly composition derived from the last election. | MVP |
| Party discipline | How reliably a party's members follow their leader's line. | V1 |
| Coalition formation | Governing requires assembling a majority from multiple parties, with a price. | V1 |
| Confidence votes | The assembly can withdraw support and bring the government down. | V1 |
| Dissolution and snap elections | Player dissolves the assembly and gambles on a better mandate. | V1 |
| Upper house | A second chamber that can delay or block. | V1 |
| Filibuster / procedural obstruction | Opposition tools to stall without the votes to defeat. | Someday |
| Committee system | Bills routed through committees that can amend or bury them. | Someday |
| Private members' bills | The assembly proposes laws *at* the player. | Someday |
| Vote buying / pork | Trade favours and local spending for individual votes. | Someday |

## Executive

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Budget authority | Set tax rates and spending lines. | MVP |
| Cabinet appointments | Ministers with competence ratings and loyalty who modify their department's output. | V1 |
| Cabinet reshuffle | Fire and replace, at a political cost. | V1 |
| Decree / executive order | Bypass the assembly on a narrow set of matters, at a legitimacy cost. | V1 |
| Emergency powers | Suspend normal process during crisis. Addictive and corrosive. | Someday |
| Civil service quality | An apparatus that executes policy well or badly, independent of intent. | Someday |
| Intelligence services oversight | Who watches them, and what they do when nobody does. | Someday |

## Constitution and system

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Political system templates | Presidential republic, parliamentary republic, constitutional monarchy, absolute monarchy, one-party state, military junta — each a data file defining institutions and powers. | V1 |
| Constitutional amendment | Change the rules of the game, usually needing a supermajority or referendum. | V1 |
| System transition | Monarchy to republic and back. The headline feature from the pitch. | V1 |
| Term limits | Self-imposed or constitutional caps on how long the player can rule. | V1 |
| Judiciary | Courts that can strike down laws. A check the player cannot simply outvote. | V1 |
| Judicial appointments | Pack the court, slowly, and change what is possible. | Someday |
| Referendums | Put a question to the population directly. High risk, high legitimacy. | V1 |
| Federalism and devolution | Powers split between national and regional government. | Someday |
| Secession crises | Regions that want out. | Someday |
| Impeachment / removal | The assembly or courts remove the player. A second lose condition. | Someday |
| Coup risk | The military decides it has had enough. | Someday |
| Succession rules | For monarchies: who inherits, and what happens when it's contested. | Someday |
