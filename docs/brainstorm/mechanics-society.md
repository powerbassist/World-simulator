# Mechanics — Society

Unfiltered candidate list. The tier column is a first-pass read; [[mvp]], [[v1]], [[someday]] and [[out-of-scope-mvp]] are the authority.

## Public opinion

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Overall approval rating | One headline number. The player's life expectancy in office. | MVP |
| Voter blocs | Population segmented into groups — workers, business owners, pensioners, students, rural, urban — each with its own approval and its own priorities. | MVP |
| Issue salience | Which topics voters currently care about, so a good policy on an ignored issue buys nothing. | MVP |
| Ideological axes | Positions on economic left/right and social liberal/conservative, for both voters and parties. | V1 |
| Opinion inertia | Sentiment that moves slowly, so no single act saves or sinks you. | MVP |
| Honeymoon and fatigue | Time in office as its own drag on approval. | V1 |
| Regional opinion | Approval varying by region rather than nationally. | Someday |
| Generational cohorts | Age groups with divergent trajectories. | Someday |

## Media and information

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Media outlets | Fictional outlets with political leanings that frame your actions. | V1 |
| Press freedom | A dial the player can turn down, with legitimacy and international costs. | V1 |
| Scandals | Revelations that damage approval and force a response. | V1 |
| Government messaging | Spend to shape the narrative around a policy. | V1 |
| State media ownership | Control the message directly. | Someday |
| Social media dynamics | Fast, ungovernable opinion swings. | Someday |
| Disinformation | Foreign and domestic. | Someday |

## Labour and unrest

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Unemployment as political input | Already modelled in [[mechanics-economy]]; here it becomes a mood driver. | MVP |
| Trade unions | Organised bodies that negotiate, endorse, and obstruct. | V1 |
| Strikes | Work stoppages that hurt output and force concessions. | V1 |
| Protests and demonstrations | Escalating unrest tied to specific grievances. | V1 |
| Riots | Where unrest goes when ignored. | Someday |
| Police response choices | Restraint or force, each with a distinct consequence. | Someday |
| General strike | The economy stops until you move. | Someday |
| Revolution | Unrest terminal state, and a lose condition. | Someday |

## Institutions and cohesion

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Corruption level | A national statistic degrading policy effectiveness and international standing. | V1 |
| Personal corruption | The player enriching themselves, with exposure risk. | Someday |
| Patronage | Buying loyalty with appointments. | Someday |
| Rule of law | Institutional strength gating what the player can get away with. | V1 |
| Regional autonomy | Devolved powers and the demands for more. | Someday |
| Separatist movements | Regions seeking exit. | Someday |
| Ethnic and linguistic minorities | Group-specific grievances and rights policy. | Someday |
| Religious institutions | Independent sources of authority and opinion. | Someday |

## Population and services

| Mechanic | Sketch | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Population size | Real starting value from [[data-sources]], changing over time. | MVP |
| Demographics and ageing | Pension and health costs on a slow, unavoidable curve. | V1 |
| Birth rate and life expectancy | Outputs of health spending and living standards. | V1 |
| Immigration and emigration | Flows responding to conditions, with strong political salience. | V1 |
| Healthcare quality | An outcome of health spending, feeding back into opinion. | V1 |
| Education quality | Slow investment with long-delayed economic returns. | V1 |
| Crime rate | Responsive to policing spend, unemployment, and inequality. | Someday |
| Inequality | A distributional statistic driving unrest independently of average prosperity. | V1 |
| Poverty rate | The floor, and the politics of where it sits. | V1 |
