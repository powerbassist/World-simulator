# V5.0 replay — the playability audit and the 10-minute script

The V5.0 play gate scored **1/5, "not ready to be played"** (author, 2026-10-20,
verbatim). This page checks each of the author's complaints against `main` at
`b466bf5d` (2026-10-21), and turns the list into a script the author can replay in
ten minutes. It follows the method of [[playability-audit]]: read the source against
the question "what does the player do next", before anything is run.

## Sources and labels

- **The author's words** come from two places only. The first is the V5.0 play-gate
  row of the V5 ledger (`.superpowers/sdd/v5-ledger.md:1034`, the replay items
  after the fix pass). The second is the playtest receiver's report
  (`.superpowers/sdd/v5.0-playtest-report.md`), which holds the first verdict's
  items P1–P14 and the replay items R1–R7. Both files are gitignored working
  notes; their line numbers are as of 2026-10-21.
- Where the author's words were recorded verbatim, they are quoted. Where the
  receiver recorded a paraphrase, the item says so.
- Every status carries a label:
  - **MEASURED**: a number from a probe or a builder's measurement, with its
    source.
  - **INFERRED**: read from the source code at the cited line. Nothing was run in
    Godot for this page.
  - **OPINION**: the designer's judgement.
- **No item below is confirmed by the author.** "Fixed" means fixed in merged code,
  read from the source and, where cited, measured by the builder. Whether the
  author agrees is what the replay is for.

## What the player does next, in the first ten minutes

A reading of today's `main`, not a play. **INFERRED** throughout unless marked.

- **Minute 0.** The game opens paused, with the term-start card. The first Space
  opens the Agenda instead of running the clock (`src/ui/main.gd:905-910`). This is
  the first decision the game asks for, and on the V5.0 build the author never
  found it (R5).
- **Minutes 1–5.** At speed 3 the clock runs 7 days a second
  (`src/core/clock.gd:12`), so a month takes about 4.3 seconds. The desk receives
  about 12 events per term (**MEASURED**: E4, 37 arrivals, 12.0 per term,
  `docs/someday.md:24`). A five-year term is 60 months, so that is one event every
  5 months, or about one every 21 seconds at speed 3. Only a major or critical
  event stops the clock (`src/ui/message_settings.gd:51`, pause at severity 2).
- **Between events**, nothing addresses the player. The levers wait for the player
  to reach for them: the budget, the laws, the diplomatic verbs and the projects.
  The people who do appear come through the same desk, as the personal-category
  events (`data/events/personal.json`), so PR B must count them in its baseline
  rather than start V5.2's audience band from zero. This is P13, and it is V5.2's
  job, not this replay's.
- **OPINION:** the gap between "a decision is asked of me" moments is the number
  that V5.2's bands should move. PR B declares it.

## The 10-minute replay

### Before you start

- **Start from the editor** (a debug build), so F12 opens the dev console
  ([[tooling]], "The in-game dev console"). Type `help` to list the commands.
- **Start a new game as France.** Every save written before V5.1-0d is refused,
  ABOBA and ABOBA2 included, because the save format moved to version 41.
- **Do not use `skip` or `term_start` to check a freeze.** Both run the month
  whole in one frame, as a test does (`src/ui/month_runner.gd:58-62`). Only real
  time passing at speed 3 shows the fix.
- For each step, note **OK** or **NOT OK**, and one sentence. A score is
  welcome but not required: this replay is a checkpoint, not the play gate.

## Proposals for other owners

The parent routes each one. Nothing here edits `src/`, `data/` or `tests/`.

1. **Interface — the held-embassy reason.** Give `_unavailable_reason()` a branch
   for the `embassy_held` precondition ("France already has an embassy in
   Zagreb."), and one for `relations_exist` ("Cyprus and Turkey have no diplomatic
   relations.", for a Cypriot player). Today both fall through to
   the generic line (`src/ui/nation_panel.gd:390`).
2. **Interface + QA — a new `tests/test_playability.gd` entry,
   `_check_greyed_verb_names_its_reason`.** For the player's nation and every
   partner, no greyed diplomatic verb should show the generic "Conditions for this
   action are not met." when one named precondition is the only failing gate. It
   fails today on `open_embassy` for every roster partner. The embassy rule is
   already pinned by `tests/test_embassies.gd`; this pins what the player is told.
3. **Systems — no change.** Nothing in this audit asks for a sim change before
   V5.2. The R5 grace window and P12's procedure are V5.3's, per the author's
   answers below.

The already-pinned fixes were checked so that nothing above duplicates them: the

## The author's answers (2026-10-21)

Four design calls went to the author through the parent session, each with the
recommended option first. The author took the recommendation on all four. The
answers are as the parent relayed them; only answer 4 came with the author's own
words.

1. **R4 — which window? The in-game panels.** Resizing them goes to V5.1d (the
   windows block), and the author confirms at replay step 9. R4 is therefore
   open, not partly fixed: the OS-window fix in PR #30 answered a reading the
   author did not mean.
2. **R5 — a programme chosen mid-term: carried to V5.3.** It is a decision-model
   call there, between two options: a grace window in the first months of a term,
   or "choose later, at a cost".
3. **P9 — the micro-states: parked** in [[someday]], with a roster expansion as
   the trigger.
4. **P12 — nationalisation: "ça doit se passer comme dans la vraie vie"** (it
   must happen as in real life; the author, verbatim). So it becomes a V5.3
   decision modelled on the real French procedure, not a cabinet click. The
   procedure, checked against the sources below on 2026-10-21:
   - **It takes a law.** Article 34 of the Constitution of 1958 puts "les
     nationalisations d'entreprises" among the matters whose rules the law sets,
     so a nationalisation needs a bill through Parliament.
   - **The Conseil constitutionnel can review it, and once struck one down.** The
     1981 nationalisation law, adopted on 18 December 1981, was referred by
     senators and deputies. Decision n° 81-132 DC (16 January 1982) accepted the
     principle but struck down the shareholders' compensation, among other
     articles, and because those articles could not be separated from the rest,
     the whole law fell. The ground was article 17 of the Declaration of 1789:
     no one may be deprived of property except "sous la condition d'une juste
     et préalable indemnité".
   - **The price was then rewritten and passed.** A revised text became loi
     n° 82-155 of 11 February 1982, which decision n° 82-139 DC (11 February
     1982) found constitutional. From the first adoption to the valid law took
     about two months.
   - **What V5.3 should model, as the author asked:** the time a bill takes; the
     price, negotiated with the shareholders or challenged by them; and the legal
     risk of a referral that can strike the law down on its compensation.
   - **INFERRED (a design note, not a source):** the game already has the pieces
     for the first and third: bills through the Legislature, and a court that
     strikes down a law (`Judiciary.strikes_down()`, `src/sim/judiciary.gd:96`;
     [[judiciary-model]]). The price, and a strike-down on the price alone, are
     the new parts: today the court reads a law's reach, not its compensation
     ([[judiciary-model]], `struck = reach(law) > tolerance(ns)`).

Sources for answer 4:
[Constitution of 4 October 1958, article 34](https://www.conseil-constitutionnel.fr/le-bloc-de-constitutionnalite/texte-integral-de-la-constitution-du-4-octobre-1958-en-vigueur);
[Declaration of 1789, article 17](https://www.conseil-constitutionnel.fr/le-bloc-de-constitutionnalite/declaration-des-droits-de-l-homme-et-du-citoyen-de-1789);
[Decision n° 81-132 DC](https://www.conseil-constitutionnel.fr/decision/1982/81132DC.htm);
[Decision n° 82-139 DC](https://www.conseil-constitutionnel.fr/decision/1982/82139DC.htm).
