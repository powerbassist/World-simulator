# V5.1 play gate — the C items, triaged

The author played V5.1-c (build `953d25cf`) on 2026-10-22 and scored it **2/5,
"way better but still unpolished"** (verbatim). This note takes the gate's
design items (G2, G5, G7, G10, G11, G14) and the answers to the tier's questions
Q1, Q2 and Q4, and says for each where it belongs: **d can take it cheaply**,
V5.2, V5.3, or later. The B items (G1, G3, G4, G6, G9, G12, G13) and G8 are
already given to d1 and to Systems in the V5.1 brief and are not triaged here.

Sources: the receiver's report (`.superpowers/sdd/v5.1-playtest-report.md`,
"Score and answers" and "Items"), the V5.1 brief's section "d -- the last
sub-block", [[v5]] and [[v5.2-spec]]. Both `.superpowers` files are gitignored
working notes, cited by section. Labels as in [[v5.0-replay]]: **MEASURED**,
**INFERRED**, **OPINION**. Nothing was run in Godot.

## The author's calls, first

Each has the recommendation first.

1. **G2 — the country box: which approval does it show for a foreign
   nation?** Today the Diplomacy panel prints another nation's true approval
   (`src/ui/nation_panel.gd:185-189`), while V5's fog rule says a figure the
   president cannot know is "shown, never silent" as a range with its source
   ([[v5]], "The three calls I would take", 1).
   (a) *Recommended:* the box shows a foreign nation's approval as a range,
   with its source ("press reports"), or omits it.
   (b) Keep the true figure, as today.
2. **G2 — does d take the country box and the right-click actions?**
   (a) *Recommended:* yes, as d4 after d1-d3: a small box on a left click and a
   right-click menu of the verbs already in the Diplomacy panel. Interface,
   about M. No sim change.
   (b) Leave it to V5.3, with the rest of the decision grammar.
3. **G5 — what is a "grand plan"?** The author's words, "lack of grand plan
   decisions", fit two different things.
   (a) A multi-year national plan (several measures, a budget envelope, a
   deadline): the Agenda's programme widened, which is V5.3b's decision
   grammar plus R5's mid-term choice, already carried to V5.3.
   (b) Large construction (a reactor fleet, a TGV line): V5.3c's construction
   register and V5.4d's lines.
   *Recommended:* ask the author which one they meant, or whether both. Both
   are already in V5.3, and the answer sets V5.3's order.
4. **G7 — where is the builder's price negotiated?**
   (a) *Recommended:* in a V5.2 audience with the builder firm's chief
   executive, with V5.2's levers (bargain, pressure, promise a date). A state
   firm builds at cost; a private one asks a price you negotiate.
   (b) In a numbers dialog (an offer, a counter-offer, the firm's answer), the
   machine V5.4d builds for cross-border lines.
5. **G11 — retune the type scale in d?**
   (a) *Recommended:* yes, a small d item: even steps between the text levels,
   the base size unchanged ("la taille du texte est ok"), checked by the
   windowed screenshot pass. Interface, S.
   (b) Later, with V5.4's map art pass.
6. **G14 / Q4 — which information felt irrelevant or badly presented?**
   *Recommended:* ask the author for two or three examples (a screen and a
   number), because the answer ends with a question mark and names nothing.
   Until then, nothing is built for G14 on its own.

## The items

### G2 — the Diplomacy panel on a country click

- **(a) The problem:** "diplomacy panel when clicking a country is useless,
  should be a small box resuming basing stats, if foreign country, basics stats
  plus actual relationship; a right click should open fast actions to take"
  (author, verbatim).
- **What it is today (INFERRED, `src/ui/nation_panel.gd`):** a full window with
  eight lines of figures (`_refresh`, lines 157-193: leader, GDP and growth,
  unemployment, debt, approval, the term) and then the relations line and every
  diplomatic verb (`_refresh_actions`). The verbs are what the player came for,
  and they sit below the figures. The map has no right-click handler
  (MEASURED: `MOUSE_BUTTON_RIGHT` appears once in `src/ui`, in
  `election_panel.gd`).
- **(b) Where it belongs: d can take it, as a separate item (M).**
  - A left click opens a small box beside the country: the name and flag, three
    or four figures, and for a foreign nation the relations score and the
    pact or agreement in force. Its "More" button opens today's Diplomacy panel.
  - A right click opens a short menu of the verbs that are available now,
    reusing `nation_panel`'s availability rule and its reasons; a greyed verb
    names its reason, as the embassy proposal in [[v5.0-replay]] asked.
  - **No sim change**: the box and the menu read state and call the commands
    that exist.
  - **INFERRED risk:** d1 already holds G6's placement work on the same map;
    the box must close when placement starts, as G6's panel does.
- **(c) Calls 1 and 2 above.**

### G5 — "lack of grand plan decisions"

- **(a) The problem:** "lack of grand plan decisions" (author, verbatim).
- **What exists (INFERRED):** the Agenda's programme, offered only on the first
  day of a term (`src/sim/programme.gd:86`; [[v5.0-replay]], R5); the five
  projects; the energy orders. None is a plan of several measures the player
  composes, sized and placed.
- **(b) Where it belongs: V5.3**, which is built for it: "decisions in real
  units, in real regions, with an estimated cost" ([[v5]], "V5.3 — Domains and
  decisions", Objective). Sub-block b gives graded and quantified decisions,
  sub-block c the construction register ("build or demolish N units of a type,
  in one region, by hand, by need or by population"), and R5's mid-term
  programme choice is already carried there.
  - **Nothing for d.** A plan without V5.3's units would be a label over the
    same levers.
- **(c) Call 3 above.**

### G7 — public or private builder, and the price

- **(a) The problem:** "depending on the installation type, if the company
  building it is public or private, the state should negociate the price with
  the company" (author, verbatim).
- **What exists (MEASURED, grep):** neither `src/sim/projects.gd` nor
  `src/sim/energy.gd` names a builder, a firm or a contractor; a project's cost
  is authored in `data/project-actions/` and `data/energy-actions/`. The firms
  exist (companies, with the state's stake, [[companies-model]] §20-22), and so
  do their chief executives (464 people of kind `executive` in `data/people/`;
  MEASURED, a data read on 2026-10-21).
- **(b) Where it belongs: V5.3c, with the negotiation from V5.2.**
  - V5.3c's construction register gives a cost "as a range set by the
    ministry's competence; overruns" ([[v5]], V5.3 c). The builder is the place
    to add: a firm, state-owned or private.
  - The negotiation is V5.2's: an audience with the firm's chief executive,
    with the levers of [[v5.2-spec]] ("The influence levers, once a topic is
    reached").
  - **OPINION:** a state firm, whose stake the government holds, builds at
    cost, and only a private one asks a price to negotiate. Nothing in the game
    or the tier doc decides this yet; it is call 4's premise.
  - V5.4d's offer-and-counter machine for a cross-border line is the
    alternative ([[v5]], V5.4 d: "the project's first negotiation machine").
  - **Nothing for d.**
- **(c) Call 4 above.**

### G10 and Q2 — little feedback on consequences

- **(a) The problem:** "j'ai eu des event mais je n'ai pas bcp de retour des
  conséquences" (author, verbatim, answering Q2 "what refusing cost").
- **What exists (INFERRED):**
  - Before the choice, each option carries its authored sentence
    (`src/ui/events_panel.gd`, `_build_choice`), and no figure.
  - After the choice, the measured effect is real but late and far away: the
    answer is queued as an effect (`events_panel._on_choose` emits `acted`;
    `main.gd`'s `_queue_effect`), measured at the History step of the next
    month close (`src/ui/main.gd`, the History branch of the month's step
    hook), and printed on the Overview's RECENT DECISIONS list, five rows long
    (`src/ui/overview_panel.gd`, `_fill_recent_effects`). The player must open
    the Overview after that close to see it; nothing else reads
    `recent_effects` (MEASURED, grep over `src/ui`).
- **(b) Where it belongs: two parts.**
  - **d can take it cheaply (S): a consequence card.** At the month close that
    measures the answer, a card in the inbox says what the answer did: the event, the
    choice and the two or three largest movements, read from the same
    `recent_effects` entry the Overview already builds. No sim change, no new
    measurement; the card's only new code is the push.
  - **V5.2: refusal priced and shown.** [[bands-v5.2]] B3 requires every
    decline to show its price on the person's card, and [[v5.2-spec]]
    pre-mortem cause 5 is exactly this complaint, for people. V5.2d gives the
    events speakers ([[v5]], V5.2 d), so a consequence can come back as a person
    who remembers.
- **(c) None for the card.** Whether d takes it is the parent's call, within
  d's budget.

### G11 — "the proportions between the different texts are odd"

- **(a) The problem:** "la taille du texte est ok, mais les proportions entre
  les differents texte est bizzare" (author, verbatim).
- **What it is today (MEASURED, `src/ui/ui_theme.gd`, the `SIZE_*` constants):**
  11, 12, 15, 18, 20, and 30 for the one hero figure. The steps between
  neighbours are uneven: 11 to 12 (+9 %) and 18 to 20 (+11 %) are nearly the
  same size, while 12 to 15 (+25 %) and 20 to 30 (+50 %) jump.
  - **INFERRED:** two pairs of levels a player cannot tell apart, beside two
    jumps, is one likely reading of "bizarre". The weights and widths that
    `ui_theme.gd` cuts from Archivo were not checked here.
- **(b) Where it belongs: d can take it cheaply (S).** Even steps around the
  base of 15 (the base stays, since the size is "ok"), applied in `ui_theme.gd`
  only, then the windowed screenshot pass on every staged state
  (`tools/screenshot.gd`) and the +35 % fit sweep (`tests/test_i18n_fit.gd`).
  The exact steps are Interface's.
- **(c) Call 5 above.**

### G14 and Q4 — information present but irrelevant or badly presented

- **(a) The problem:** "j'ai des infos, mais pas forcément pertinentes ou mal
  présentées ?" (author, verbatim, answering Q4 "what you do not know, and
  why").
- **(b) Where it belongs: mostly V5.1-d and V5.3, once the author names
  examples.**
  - d re-homes the 22 windows as domain tabs ([[v5]], V5.1 d), which is where
    presentation is fixed screen by screen.
  - d3's term-text sweep (the V5.1 brief, d3) makes figures explainable on hover
    ([[glossary-tooltips]]).
  - V5.3b's statistics service sets "how precise every table is", which is the
    fog half of Q4: knowing *that* you do not know ([[v5]], V5.3 b).
  - **INFERRED, a related find:** the Diplomacy panel shows a foreign nation's
    true approval (call 1), a figure the player should not have. A screen that
    shows what it should hide can read as irrelevant precision.
- **(c) Call 6 above.**

### Q1 — "nobody physical asked me for anything, only events"

- **(a) The problem:** "aucune persone physique ne m'a demandé quelque chose,
  seulement des évents" (author, verbatim).
- **(b) Where it belongs: V5.2, by design.** This is V5.2's whole objective
  ("People ask to see you, talk, bargain, lie and remember", [[v5]], V5.2), and
  [[v5.2-spec]] names the same baseline: today the only voices that reach the
  desk are the personal-category events ("What the player has today"). Band
  B1 ([[bands-v5.2]]) is what V5.2 must meet: a first request by month 3 and at
  least 8 in months 1–20.
  - **Nothing for d.** The answer is the expected V5.1 result, not a defect:
    V5.1 is the shell, and V5.2 is the people.
- **(c) None.**
