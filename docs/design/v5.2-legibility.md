# V5.2-0b — the text diet, panel by panel

V5.1 closed at 2/5. The author's closing sentence, verbatim: "2/5 the game lack
soul; some panels are really monolithic and uneasy to read, oversized buttons
(politics button disolve for example), i think i need graph, diagrams, to make
data more digest". The same gate's Q4 examples: "still a lot of useless text
everywhere", "legislature tab is clunky and heavy", "government - politics, meme
probleme dans energie, le boutton est super large !" (`.superpowers/sdd/v5.1-gate2-report.md`,
"Score and answers" and "Gate 2b answers").

This note is Gameplay's keep/cut list for sub-block 0b (the V5.2 brief, "0 --
legibility"). For every workspace tab in `src/ui/domains.gd` it gives what the
panel says today, measured, what a player needs from it, what to cut, what to move
into a glossary term or a plate, where a chart replaces text, and a prose budget
Interface's test can pin. Interface builds 0b from it. The chart kit is 0a's;
this note only says which chart, where, and whether the game already records its
series.

Labels: **MEASURED** (the probe below, or a cited line), **INFERRED** (read from
the code or the author's words, not run), **OPINION** (the designer's call).

## The author's calls

Three calls, each with the recommendation first. Interface can build everything
else in this note without them.

1. **The Legislature's catalogue: one line per law, with the detail on select?**
   - **(a) Recommended:** each proposable law is one row: its name, a theme chip,
     a majority chip and the forecast as a small stacked bar with the majority
     mark. Selecting a row opens a detail pane with the summary, the Senat and the
     court, and the verbs (Propose, Force, Referendum). The list drops from about
     3 850 words to about 700 (MEASURED today; the target is INFERRED).
   - (b) Keep every row expanded and only cut the repeated Senat and court
     sentence. That saves 1 219 words (MEASURED) and leaves about 2 630: still the
     heaviest panel in the game, three times Orgs (845 words at month 12).
   - (c) Group the list by theme, collapsed by default. Same weight as (a) once a
     group is open, and one more click to reach any law.
2. **Authored blurbs (the law summaries, the programme texts): always visible,
   or on select?** They are the game's written voice, which "soul" asks for more
   of, not less.
   - **(a) Recommended:** on select. The list shows the name and the figures; the
     blurb is the first thing in the detail pane, at full length, never cut.
   - (b) Always visible, as today: 1 325 words on the Legislature, 1 115 on the
     Agenda's first day (MEASURED).
3. **The constitution block in Government > Politics: a plate, or stays inline?**
   Eleven lines (MEASURED, nodes 5–6 and 14–22 of the dump) describe rules that
   do not change unless a law changes them; nodes 7–13 between them are the live
   seat arithmetic, which stays.
   - **(a) Recommended:** a "How this republic works" plate, folded by default,
     with each rule's term in the glossary. The live arithmetic (seats,
     majority, censure, rebels) stays on the panel as a seat bar.
   - (b) Stays inline, with section headings added.

## How the text was counted

**MEASURED at `dc515826`** (origin/main, PR #90 merged). A scratch probe, kept
in Gameplay's scratchpad and not committed, did this:

- It opened every registered window as `tests/test_i18n_fit.gd` does:
  `main.tscn` instanced, the window manager given 1920 × 1080, then for each id
  `windows.open(id, true)`, two frames, and a walk of `frame._content`.
- It counted every VISIBLE `Label`, `RichTextLabel` (`get_parsed_text()`) and
  `Button`. A hidden subtree is skipped.
- A word is a whitespace token containing a letter, so `318`, `51%` and `+0.00`
  are not words, and `pts` is.
- It ran at two stages, France, idle, nobody clicking:
  - **day 1**;
  - **month 12**: twelve `WorldMonth.tick_month()` calls on the live state, then
    `mark_all_dirty()`.
- **Limit:** the month-12 stage skips `main.gd`'s step hook, so the inbox, the
  Agenda's programme offer and the Diary are not in their played state at month 12.
  Read the Agenda at day 1 and Dispatches as a lower bound.

**Two kinds of text, budgeted differently** (OPINION, and the basis of every
budget below):

- **System prose:** sentences the code writes about how the game works,
  through `tr()` in `src/ui`. This is the "useless text everywhere". It is
  budgeted in absolute words per panel.
- **Content:** prose from `data/` through `Strings`: law summaries, programme
  blurbs, event consequences, council proposals. This is the voice "soul" asks
  for. It is budgeted per visible row, never as a panel total, because the total
  grows with content.
- **How the split was made:** a text node is CONTENT if it contains a string of
  five or more words found in `data/**/*.json`, and SYSTEM otherwise. This is
  exact for summaries and blurbs. It is approximate for templated rows: a
  formatted row with a data name inside it counts as system.

**The budget unit is system SENTENCE words:** the words in system text nodes of
eight words or more. A table cell, a heading or a figure label is not a sentence,
and does not count. The unit is the "sys 8+" column below. Interface's test can
pin it per panel with the same walk.

**What the table says** (INFERRED from it):

- **The Legislature alone carries 1 499 of the game's ~3 230 system sentence
  words, 46 %.**

  already sit at 20 sentence words or fewer, and the cuts are structural: one
  panel rebuilt, three trimmed. Elsewhere, a plain line or two.

## The three panels the author named

### Parliament > Legislature — "clunky and heavy"

**Today** (MEASURED, day 1 and month 12 identical):

- 421 text nodes, 3 850 words and 210 buttons.
- The panel is `src/ui/law_panel.gd`:
  - `_refresh()` (`:146-189`) lists every proposable law;
  - `_build_law_row()` (`:381-458`) builds each row expanded.
- 105 laws, each a block of about six nodes:
  - the name row, `name (theme, majority)`, 6 words (`:385`);
  - the summary, 5–22 words of content (`:397`);
  - the Senat and court sentence, 5–16 words (`_after_the_division()`,
    `:557-583`);
  - the forecast, 4–8 words (`:403`);
  - two or three buttons.
- **The worst repeat:** a 5–16-word Senat and court sentence on all 105 rows,
  1 219 words (MEASURED), built from the same six templates (`:565-580`).
- **No overview.** The seats and the majority line are not on this panel at all.
  The page opens on the first law (dump node 1) and is all list.

**What a player needs** (OPINION): "which of these can I pass, and what will it
cost me". Today that answer sits in the forecast and the Senat lines, buried in
the fourth node of every block.

**Keep:**

- the referendum and held-bill blocks at the top (`:162-169`): they are in
  flight, and the only thing the list cannot say;
- the assembly's in-tray (`_build_assembly_section`, `:618`);
- the result summary of the last vote (`:154-155`);
- every verb.

**Cut:**

- The Senat and court sentence from the row. It becomes two glyphs on the row:
  - Senat: agree, too close, delay N months, or block;
  - court: stands or strikes;
  - the full sentence is the glyph's tooltip, and sits in the detail pane.
- The theme and majority from the name's parentheses: they become chips.
- "No law is currently proposable" stays.

**Move to the glossary or a plate** (ids already in `data/glossary/terms.json`):

- `second_chamber`, `article_49_3`, `amendment`, `majority`, `referendum`,
  `censure` (MEASURED: the file holds all six).
- MISSING, proposed to Content: `constitutional_court`, which is the court's
  0.38 bar, read on 71 rows today (every row but the constitutional ones) and
  explained nowhere.

**Chart** (all from live state; no history needed):

- **Per row:** the forecast as a stacked bar (for / undecided / against) with the
  majority mark, in place of "Forecast: 259 for · 210 against · 108 undecided".
  The numbers are already computed by `_forecast_text()` (`:539`).
- **At the top:** the seat bar (the same chart as Politics, below), so the page
  says the majority before it lists anything.

**Structure:**

- A list on the left, the detail on the right (call 1a).
- The list is filterable by theme, the detail open on the selected law.
- 0c's 140 ms open frame (QA pre-gate 2, C8) is 0c's to measure. One row per law
  builds fewer nodes than today's six-node block, which should help it (INFERRED).

**Budget:**

- List view: 0 system sentences per row.
- Detail pane: 80 sys 8+ words for the selected law.
- Content: the summary, one per selected law.

## Proposals for other owners

  - Pin each tab's sys 8+ ceiling from the table above, with the walk described in
    "How the text was counted". It should be Interface's own test file (this
    note's probe is scratch).

  - `constitutional_court`;
  - `import_dependency` and `emissions`;
  - `qualified_majority` and `veto`;
  - `campaign_effort`.
  - Also the "How this republic works" plate text, if call 3a is taken.
