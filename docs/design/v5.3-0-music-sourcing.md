# V5.3-0 — music sourcing: the CC0 candidate list

**Status: a list, not a download (2026-10-31).** Nothing here is in the repository. QA checks each licence against the page, the parent gives the go, and only then does Content fetch the files. Route and reason: section 4.3 of the block's spec ([[v5.3-0-readability]]): ElevenLabs Music is out for the public repository because of its Music Library clause, so CC0 or public domain.

**How the list was made.** OpenGameArt pages read in a real browser on 2026-10-31 (the site refuses scripted fetches). For each track: the title, the author and the licence field are as the page shows them; the page's licence field is the uploader's own declaration, which is the most a CC0 source can give. No file was listened to by a person yet, and durations are not on the pages: they are measured on download. Every bed must be at least 2 minutes (spec 4.3); a shorter loop is repeated at least to that length by the player, so a short loop here still qualifies if it loops without a seam, which the listening check decides.

**What is not on the list on purpose.** Anything CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, GPL or "free for commercial use" without a CC0 or public-domain mark (Kevin MacLeod's library, Tsorthan Grove's "Eyes in the Shadows" at CC-BY 4.0, "Lose Music #1" at GPL / CC-BY-SA): attribution is a different row of the REUSE table and the spec asked for none.

## Licence and REUSE

- Every file above is declared CC0 by its uploader. `REUSE.toml` needs one annotation per file or per folder with the same source: `SPDX-License-Identifier = "CC0-1.0"` and `SPDX-FileCopyrightText` naming the author and the page URL. `LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt` does not exist yet (`reuse download CC0-1.0` adds it); it must land in the same PR as the first CC0 annotation, because `reuse lint` fails on an unused licence; `LicenseRef-PublicDomain` is the existing alternative and is less exact for a CC0 dedication, so CC0-1.0 is proposed.
- Transcoding to OGG is a derivative, which CC0 allows; the annotation keeps the source URL beside it. The 40 MB WAVs never enter the repository.
- Provenance per file goes in `assets/audio/music/manifest.json` (author, page URL, licence as read, date read, file hash), the shape of `assets/audio/voice/manifest.json`.
- Risk to name: a CC0 declaration on an upload site is the uploader's word. A track that turns out to be someone else's work is the uploader's breach, but it is still in the repository. QA's check on each page (author history, the file's own tags) is the mitigation; nothing stronger exists for this route.

## The two synthesized stings (parent: yes, 2026-10-31; Interface builds them with the music player)

- **Vote failed:** about 2 seconds, a falling minor third on a soft low string-like tone, no resolution, a short dry tail. Sober, not tragic: a bill lost is a setback, not a death. Quieter than the passed sting.
- **Election lost:** about 4 seconds, a slow falling line that comes to rest on an open fifth, a low drone under it fading out. Grave and final, with no bitterness and no "game over" cliché. Louder and longer than the failed vote, so the two never sound alike.
- Both follow `sting.gd`: synthesized in code, no licence, no attribution file.
