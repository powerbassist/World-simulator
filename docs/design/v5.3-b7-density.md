# V5.3 — band B7 at 100 % of months

**Status: accepted 2026-11-10, docs only; the author's five calls are answered in section 10, each on the recommendation.** Nothing here is built. The author asked for a meaningful decision in 100 % of months before his play gate (band B7, [[v5.3-ab]] section 7); today it is 58 to 67 %, depending on nation and regime (`.superpowers/sdd/v5.3-pregate-report.md`, B7's table). The figures below come from `tools/decision_surfaces_probe.gd --density` on `main` at `1b100e46` and from a throwaway prototype in a detached worktree, deleted after the runs (section 9).

Labels as elsewhere: **MEASURED** (a probe output or a source line), **INFERRED** (reasoned from what was measured), **OPINION** (a design proposal).

## 1. The recommendation

**Fill the holes with what the game already has, and add no new system.** Four changes:

1. **Surface three decisions that exist and that no card or briefing line points at:** a council vote your vote can actually swing, a backbench bill awaiting your stand, a treaty awaiting your assembly. Each is a quiet card that never holds the clock.
2. **Count the fourth, which already has a card:** the Regions arrival. The probe does not read it.
3. **Add one rule, the quiet-month call.** If a month ends with no arrival at all, the game calls up one thing that is already waiting: a person with a want (a request never holds the clock), up to nine asks in twelve months, then a minor event whose answers really differ.
4. **Lower one clause of the band to what the content can honestly carry:** a mean of at least 1.4 a month instead of 2 (section 6, the author's call; the lowest run, GBR idle, measures 1.49).

**The result, MEASURED on the prototype (section 5):** every run reaches **96 to 100 %** of months (four nations, both regimes, up to 120 months), with a mean of 1.49 to 1.99. All eight runs meet the spec's measured fallback (at least one in 90 % of months, never two empty in a row). France and Germany reach 100 %; Britain reaches 96 to 97 %, Poland active 96 %. The months still empty are ones where the called item was dull, which a stricter filter is expected to close (section 7, not yet measured).

**B7's four clauses, honestly.** The share clause, which is the author's, is reachable. The "mean at least 2" clause is reached by no run, and I found no source that would honestly carry it (section 6). The "at most 6 a month" clause fails in one month of 69 in each Polish run once the existing sources are counted: POL active fails today too, POL idle is new (MEASURED; the call alone leaves both at 6 or fewer). The "3 domains a quarter" clause rises from 25-39 % of quarters to 62.5-100 %, and does not reach 100 %. So `--density` still exits 1 until the author rules on section 6.

## 2. Where the holes are (MEASURED)

Across QA's eight runs, 247 months are empty.

- **They are ordinary months.** 210 of the 247 have an empty desk and 200 have no request waiting. Only 4 are election months and 7 the month after one, so the spec's worry (the first month of a term, a government forming, [[v5.3-ab]] section 7) is not where the holes are. Nothing is seasonal: across the runs, empties fall in every calendar month. The worst stretch is seven months running (FRA active, POL active).
- **The game does not lack content in those months.** On average 58 to 73 events pass every gate in an empty month (24 to 35 of them minor), against a monthly hazard sum of 0.18 to 0.27, and every empty month had at least one eligible event. The world has things to say and draws rarely.
- **Decisions that exist land in the holes and are not counted.** In these 247 months (a month can hold several):

The called event's weakness is its size. That is why the person comes first and the event only after the nine-asks cap.

**The rule, written out (OPINION).** When a month ends and nothing arrived in it (no event delivered, no request, no closure announced, no sector warning, no firm bid or failure, no process ask, no council the player can swing), the game takes one thing. A bill awaiting a stand, a treaty and the Regions arrival do not stop the call: the prototype did not read them, and section 5's figures assume that (Systems' call if the build wants them read, a small move down in the mean):

- a person who may ask and has an unserved want, the one with the highest standing, if fewer than nine people asked in the last twelve months and the lane is not full;
- otherwise a minor event, drawn by likelihood among those whose gates pass and whose choices differ on at least one arrow or on money.

**The shipping shape (the parent's ruling, 2026-11-10, on QA's call on #315).** The fill is in the same month, and no existing step moves in `WorldMonth.STEPS`:

- **(a) The person.** At the end of `Audiences.tick_month`, which already runs after Events, Closures, Companies, Sectors and Processes, so it sees the whole month. It reads the month's arrival stamps off `GameState` and stamps whether it called someone.
- **(b) The event.** One new step owned by Events, `QuietEvent` (`Events.quiet_call`), placed right after `Audiences` and before `Elections`. It reads the same stamps, Audiences' call stamp included, and delivers one filtered minor event only if nothing arrived. Events never calls Audiences. The step goes into `STEPS`, its row into `GROUPS` in `tools/vault_diagrams.py` (run with `--write`), and `tests/test_world_month.gd` follows. Its draw is part of the re-baseline the author accepted in principle (call 5); no other step moves.
- **(c) The council swing.** Orgs stamps the swing into its own register at the session's opening; the rule and the card read that stamp, never an Orgs read from outside.
- **(d) The arrival stamp.** On the desk entry, not the tick report's list (that list is the step hook's and the UI's). An absent stamp reads as none: no save bump.

This is the prototype's order: it called the person and then the event at the end of the Audiences step, which is where the new step sits, so section 5's figures stand for this shape (INFERRED: the code differs, the order and the month do not). The rule reads the registers the month already wrote, through `GameState`, never the UI, and adds no text and no content.

## 6. Why not a mean of two

The band's "mean at least 2 a month" is reached by no run: 1.49 to 1.99.

- [[bands-v5.2]] B1 already drew the line. Of 48 requests plus 12 events a term (about one a month), it says "More than that is a flood." A mean of 2 is twice that line.
- At the author's pace of about 74 seconds a month, two arrivals a month is one every 37 seconds, on top of the monthly briefing, and a real player also tables bills and opens processes that the probe's active regime does not (section 9).
- The sources that could carry a second arrival a month (budget season, polls, addresses, the stages of processes the player opens) follow the player's own rhythm. They are not holes for the world to fill.

**My number (OPINION):** at least one meaningful decision every month, a mean of at least 1.4, at most 6 a month, 3 domains in at least 60 % of quarters (the runs: 62.5 to 100 %). That is all of what the author asked ("Une par mois") and it does not fight B1. These are one seed per nation (section 9): GBR idle measures 1.49 and a second seed could land under 1.4, so the first re-baseline should re-read the mean before the number is signed.

## 7. What is left, and what could go wrong

- **The months still empty.** In the table, every month still empty (Britain, Poland active) holds one arrival the probe judged dull. The prototype's filter is coarser than `DensityCount.differs` (MEASURED: `dull` is non-zero in each). Filtering on the probe's own rule is INFERRED to reach 100 %, and is not measured. If it does not, the honest number is 96 %.
- **A called event never holds.** The author kept the lapse guard for minor decisions on 2026-10-22 ([[decision-surfaces]], call 4), and this proposes one exception: an event the game called up in a quiet month waits, and lapses with a note. Without it, the idle regime pays up to 2.3 more holding cards a year (DEU 1.9 → 4.2, inside the 6; the event-first version broke it).
- **Pre-mortem: "something to do" was met by noise.** The call is the row most likely to earn that line. The guards are the person first, the 12-month cap, the filter on real differences. If the author reads it as noise at the gate, the event branch goes first and the rule stays; that version was not measured (no run had the cap without the event branch), and it reaches at most what the existing sources reach plus nine asks a year.
- **The balance moves.** FRA active ends at 76 months instead of 120 and GBR idle at 100 instead of 120 on the prototype (MEASURED): called events and requests move approval and a defeat comes earlier. Systems writes the re-baseline; every moved number is the author's to accept. `tools/balance_parallel.py` was not run (the brief reserves it for the parent's go).
- **E4 and B1 move.** E4 today is 12 events a term, the call takes it to 16 to 22; B1's 48 is touched at the upper end in Germany, France and Poland (the author's, section 10).
- **RF2 fails in 3 of 8 briefing runs on the prototype and 1 of 8 on `main`** (MEASURED, `--briefing`), with "qualified never shown" naming `person` or `budget`: three lines are not enough for every kind in a fuller month. Two runs worse than base is a real cost, for Interface to read at the build.

## 9. What could not be verified

- **One seed per nation.** The draws are deterministic per game, and a player's real seed differs (`draw_seed`). Each row is one sample, as QA's table is.
- **The active regime is a model.** It answers every card, delegates every request, opens one process a year and tables no bill, so it undercounts what a player who legislates sees.
- **The prototype is not the shipping shape.** It read a private function and a static; the build must not (section 8). The prototype's diff is in the PR body.
- **Nothing here was played.** Whether a called request feels like a person or like a slot being filled is the gate's question, and the author's.
- **The balance outcomes** were read off the runs' lengths; the re-baseline table was not run.

## 10. Author's calls

**Answered 2026-11-10 (relayed by the parent), all five on the recommendation:**

1. B7 is at least one meaningful decision every month, a mean of at least 1.4, at most 6 a month, 3 domains in at least 60 % of quarters. The mean is re-read at the first re-baseline.
2. The quiet-month call: yes. A person first (at most nine asks in twelve months), then a filtered minor event.
3. A called event never holds the clock: it waits and lapses to a note. The lapse guard is unchanged for every other minor event.
4. Council: a card only when the player's vote swings the result; the rest stay a mark on the tab.
5. The balance moves are accepted in principle. Systems' exact re-baseline table goes to the author before the build merges.

The questions as they were put:

Une recommandation par question, la première option est celle que je conseille.

1. **Le chiffre.** Tu veux au moins une vraie décision chaque mois ? Avec la proposition, on atteint 96 à 100 % des mois selon le pays. Je conseille d'accepter : au moins une décision par mois, une moyenne d'au moins 1,4 par mois au lieu de 2, au plus 6 par mois, 3 domaines dans au moins 60 % des trimestres (une seule graine par pays : la Grande-Bretagne mesure 1,49, une autre graine pourrait passer sous 1,4, on relit le chiffre au prochain tableau). On peut garder la moyenne de 2, mais aucun essai ne l'atteint et ça ferait deux fois ce que le projet a lui-même appelé un déluge : il faudrait alors une nouvelle source, à définir.

2. **Le mois calme.** Le jeu peut-il faire venir quelqu'un, ou un petit événement, quand rien n'arrive ? Je conseille oui : d'abord une personne qui veut quelque chose, jusqu'à neuf par an, ensuite un petit événement dont les réponses diffèrent vraiment. Sans cette règle, on plafonne à 66-77 %. Si ça te paraît du remplissage en jouant, on enlève la partie événement ; la règle reste, mais ce cas n'a pas été mesuré et on perd des mois.

3. **Un petit événement appelé peut-il ne jamais arrêter le temps ?** Je conseille oui : il attend, et s'il expire il devient une note. En octobre tu as gardé l'arrêt au dernier mois pour les événements mineurs ; ce serait une exception, pour ceux que le jeu fait venir seulement. Sans cette exception, un joueur qui ne répond à rien aurait jusqu'à 2,3 cartes bloquantes de plus par an, ce qui reste dans ton budget de 6.

4. **Les votes au Conseil.** On montre seulement ceux où ton vote change le résultat ? Je conseille oui. Les autres restent dans l'onglet, sans carte : voter fait quand même bouger un peu ta relation avec le pays qui propose, mais le résultat ne change pas, et une carte pour ça, c'est du bruit.

5. **Les chiffres qui bougent.** Tu acceptes que l'équilibre change ? Avec la règle, la France en jeu actif perd au mois 76 au lieu de tenir 120 mois, et la Grande-Bretagne sans action au mois 100 au lieu de 120. Les événements passent de 12 à 16-22 par mandat, les demandes vont jusqu'à 59 par mandat pour un plafond de 48 sur les seules demandes intérieures. Je conseille d'accepter le principe ; Systems te donne le tableau exact avant de fusionner.
