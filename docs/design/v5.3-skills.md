# V5.3 — skills: one talent per domain, 0 to 20

**Status: proposed (2026-10-26), docs only.** Nothing here is built. Each band in
section 9 is declared before any build.

The author's ask, verbatim (the question tool, 2026-10-26, while accepting
c1c's numbers):

1. "Le pourcentage de chance de découverte doit venir du talent du dirigeant
   incarné dans ce domaine, l'intrigue non ?"
2. "Je voudrais un vrai talent intrigue et aussi pour les autres domaines
   diplôme ainsi etc j'aimerais bien qu'il y ait aussi des compétences un peu tu
   vois des des compétences notées de 0 et le mets moins que rien et ça peut
   aller jusqu'à 20 par exemple ou ça peut dépasser je sais pas c'est toi qui
   vois". "Diplôme" is a speech-to-text slip for "diplomatie".
3. Placement: "V5.3, conception dès maintenant (Recommended)".

Read as: several skills, one per domain (intrigue, diplomacy and the others),
each rated from 0 ("less than nothing") to 20, perhaps beyond. The scale is
Gameplay's to propose.

Labels: **MEASURED** means a cited line, a probe or a count over `data/`.
**INFERRED** means read from the code or derived, but not run. **OPINION**
means the designer's call. Source lines are pinned to `701d28f8`.

## 1. What exists (MEASURED)

**The skill field today.**
- Every person record can carry one `social_skill` in [0, 1], "reading and
  deceiving" ([[people-model]], the V5.2a registers table;
  `src/core/person.gd:63`). An absent value reads 0.5.
- Of **2,149 people in 41 files**, **324** author `social_skill`, in 0.34 to
  0.82, mean 0.60. They split as 269 of the 301 met cast (`bench: false`)
  plus 55 bench people.
  - All 41 heads of state or government are in the met cast and author
    `social_skill`, between 0.42 and 0.80.
  - The four playable leaders: France's Moucron 0.53, Britain's Marchbanks
    0.62, Germany's Vogler 0.62, Italy's Bagnasco 0.66.
  - The "1595 people" in `person_card.gd`'s header is stale.

**Who reads it today.** Mostly the person being persuaded, not the leader:
- the listener's doubt of a promised date (`lever_odds.gd:49`);
- the listener's resistance to flattery (`lever_odds.gd:59`);
- the flatter backfire (`levers.gd:328`);
- the lie catch, which reads the listener against the leader
  (`lies.gd:25-26`, called at `:53`). **The catch is the only place the
  leader's own skill is read.**

**Promised, but no reader yet.**
- [[v5-reflection]] says social skill "sets how fast you read someone". Nothing
  does: knowledge is gained at a flat rate, scaled only by what is not yet
  known (`levers.gd:341-345`, `:426`).
- V5.3b's address to the nation moves opinion "by the president's social skill
  and standing" ([[v5]], V5.3 b). This spec claims that hook.

**Exposure reads no skill.** The author's first point is exactly this gap:
hazard = RATE × reach × modifier (`exposure.gd:69`, `:121`).

**No person holds a portfolio.**
- `ns.cabinet[portfolio]` names a party. A per-person minister register was
  never written (`game_state.gd:164`), and the resign act says "there is no
  person-in-portfolio slot" (`characters.gd:497-499`).
- Effectiveness is the party's `competence` × its affinity to the portfolio,
  as 1 + `COMPETENCE_SPAN` (0.75) × (competence − 0.5)
  (`cabinet.gd:109-116`, `cabinet_constants.gd:45`).

**Discipline.** The party's authored figure is the ceiling. The model only
erodes it, through `APPROVAL_TERM` 0.50 and `ELECTION_TERM` 0.20
(`discipline_constants.gd:34`, `:39`), and inertia pulls it back.

**Knowledge comes only from audience topics.** No summit and no foreign act
teaches the president anything about another leader. So every foreign leader
stays a stranger (B4's widest fog) for the whole game.

## 2. The scale

- **0 to 20, with 10 as today's middling.** 10 is `social_skill` 0.5, which is
  `SKILL_MIDDLING` (`lever_constants.gd:44`). Every formula below gives
  today's number at 10. Write s′ = (skill − 10) / 10, so s′ is −1 at 0, 0 at
  10 and +1 at 20.
- **Data carries one decimal; the screen shows an integer and a word.**
  Migration reads `intrigue` = 20 × `social_skill` exactly, so France's
  authored people keep their byte-identical B5, B8 and L2 rows. Rounding
  would move them. The words:
  - 0 to 3, *hopeless* ("moins que rien": below middling, the skill does
    harm);
  - 4 to 7, *weak*;
  - 8 to 12, *able*;
  - 13 to 16, *gifted*;
  - 17 to 20, *exceptional*;
  - above 20, *legendary*;
  - below 0, *disastrous*.
- **Beyond 20, and below 0 (OPINION).** Authored and derived values stay in
  [0, 20]. Only play can push a skill past either end, through the modifier
  register (section 5), down to −5 or up to 25.
  - Past the ends each extra point is worth half:
    f(s′) = s′ for |s′| ≤ 1, and sign(s′) × (1 + (|s′| − 1) / 2) beyond.
    So 25 acts like 22.5. That makes "past 20" real but rare, earned in play
    rather than authored, and never a jump.
- **Every effect is a rule over the skill, never an id** (rule 1). Each span
  is sized so that 20 against 10 is worth about one existing step of the
  system it touches, and section 9's bands test that.

## 3. The five skills, and what each moves

A skill is listed only when something exists, or V5.3's signed sub-blocks
build something, that reads it. **Command (defence) is left out until V6's
war machine reads it**, because a skill nothing reads is the "matters
nowhere" failure.

| Skill (French) | Who carries it | What it moves (rule; at 0 / 10 / 20) |
|---|---|---|
| **Intrigue** (intrigue) | The incarnated leader for hiding and reading; any person for reading | Hiding, reading lies, reading people (below) |
| **Persuasion** (éloquence) | The incarnated leader | Audience odds; the address to the nation |
| **Diplomacy** (diplomatie) | Each nation's leader, AI nations included | Foreign acts and agreements; summits teach |
| **Parliament** (manœuvre parlementaire) | The head of government (`pm_person_id`), else the leader | The coalition's discipline erosion |
| **Administration** (gestion) | The head of government, until a minister register exists (call 4) | Portfolio effectiveness; V5.3c's cost estimates |

**Intrigue.**
- **The author's point 1.** The hazard of the press, the courts and the
  auditors is multiplied by (1 − 0.5 × f(s′)) for the sitting leader: ×1.5 at
  0, ×1.0 at 10, ×0.5 at 20. It covers every evidence row the leader left: a
  bribe, an offer, an affair, C6's overspend.
  - **A turned person is not covered (OPINION):** a witness talks whatever
    the leader's craft. That keeps c1c's lesson whole: the defence against
    the turned route is not crossing them.
  - France at 10.6 gives ×0.97, a few percent on S1 (INFERRED).
  - **A habit stays found (OPINION):** a route at its `HAZARD_MAX` of 0.25
    is still at 0.25 after the multiplier, whenever 0.5 × the raw hazard
    stays above the cap. So skill hides a single act and slows a habit, but
    it cannot make a habit safe. That is B6's "always ≥ 80 %" kept (GLM's
    finding 11, read as intended).
- **Lies, on both sides.** The catch is 0.33 + 0.75 × (reader − liar) / 20,
  today's formula in the new units. A listener's doubt and flatter read the
  listener's intrigue / 20.
- **Reading people** (the reflection's unbuilt ask). Every knowledge gain is
  × (1 + 0.5 × f(s′)) of the president: half again at 20, half at 0.

**Persuasion.**
- Argue, promise a date and flatter gain + 0.10 × f(s′) odds, re-clamped.
  Pressure (standing and approval) and bargain (a price) do not read it.
  - **Why 0.10:** half of what a believed lie (`LIE_ODDS` 0.20, c1c) or aid
    buys. The best orator gets honestly half of what a lie or a bribe buys
    (GLM's finding 8: the first draft cited the old 0.10).
- V5.3b's address moves opinion × (1 + 0.5 × f(s′)). Persuasion replaces
  "social skill" in that sub-block's text, and its standing term stays as
  written (GLM's finding 12).

**Diplomacy.**
- An act's relations delta is × (1 + 0.25 × f(s′)) of the acting nation's
  leader (`diplomacy.gd`, execute's `relations`).
- `accept_score` gains a fourth term, + `ACCEPT_SKILL_WEIGHT` × f(s′) of the
  proposing leader (`foreign_policy.gd:279-289`). Systems sets its size against
  the three existing weights with `foreign_policy_probe`, and section 9
  bounds it.
  - It is never named as a refusal reason, because the player cannot act on
    it.
- **A summit held teaches** `KNOW_DINNER` (0.10) of the partner's leader, so
  their skills' fog narrows (section 7).

**Parliament.**
- For the governing coalition's parties, the two erosion terms are
  × (1 − 0.5 × f(s′)) of the head of government. At 20 an unpopular
  government loses half the cohesion; at 0, half again more.
- The authored figure stays the ceiling, so the bound holds by construction.

**Administration.**
- Every held portfolio's effectiveness gains + 0.10 × f(s′) of the head of
  government, beside the party's competence term.
  - **Why two numbers (OPINION):** competence is the team, administration is
    the one who runs it. 0.10 is under a seventh of `COMPETENCE_SPAN`'s
    swing, so the party still matters most.
- V5.3c's construction cost estimate is × (1 − 0.5 × f(s′)) wide, and so is
  its mean overrun. This claims the sub-block's "cost as a range set by the
  ministry's competence".

## 4. Who carries which: the office does the job

The incarnated leader is the player's person (`President.leader_id`).
- **Intrigue, persuasion and diplomacy** are the leader's own: the author's
  "le talent du dirigeant incarné".
- **Parliament and administration** belong to the head of government. In
  France that is the Prime Minister, who manages the Assembly and the
  ministries. Where the leader heads the government, it is the same person.
- **The minister slot is call 4.** A per-portfolio minister would carry
  administration per ministry. That is a new save field (a bump, under the
  "a missing field reads as something wrong" rule), new content, and a change
  to `cabinet.gd`'s party-held mechanism. It is too large to take as a
  default.

## 5. `social_skill` is replaced, and the save

- **A data migration, not a save migration.**
  - A `tools/` script writes `skills.intrigue` = 20 × `social_skill` for the
    324 people who author it.
  - The loader then refuses `social_skill`, and a lint fails it in `data/`.
  - Every reader moves to `Skills.of(person, "intrigue") / 20`.
- **No save field for the skills.** They are content on `Person`.
- **One state register for play:** `ns.person_skill_delta`, {person id: {skill:
  delta}}, holding modifiers.
  - Each person's delta is capped at ±5 per skill, so an effective value
    stays in [−5, 25].
  - Absent reads 0, which is correct for every older save, so it is
    `absent_ok` and earns no bump.
- **What moves a skill in play (call 5).** An event effect key,
  `skill_delta`, through the applier. Content authors none in V5.3 unless the
  author asks. The mechanism costs one key; the register stays empty
  otherwise.

## 7. Fog: what the player sees

- **Another person's skill is a range**, through the knowledge register,
  with B4's grammar:
  - width = 20 × `LeverOdds.fog_width(knowledge)`: **7.2 points** for a
    stranger, **1.6** once known;
  - the seeded chain of `shown_range`, keyed `skill:<s>`, so each window sits
    inside the last at a fixed truth, and the truth is inside 100 %.
- **A modifier moves the truth mid-game.** The window then rides it, as the
  odds' window rides the odds. It is still inside, and still no wider than
  the knowledge allows.
- **The incarnated leader's own skills are exact.** Everyone else, the Prime
  Minister included, is read by knowledge.
- **The windows are not saved.** `shown_range` walks its chain from the seed
  and the knowledge at every read (`lever_odds.gd`, `below()`), so a load
  rebuilds the same window. No field is added (GLM's finding 3 dropped).
- **Repeated lies sample the catch.** A player who lies to one person again
  and again could estimate their intrigue from how often they are caught.
  Each catch costs −0.5 rapport, a grudge and half the knowledge, so the
  price is the guard, as it is for every fogged odds, where repeated attempts
  sample the truth too (GLM's finding 1, recorded, not blocked).
- **Foreign leaders:** a summit now teaches (section 3), so a met partner
  narrows from 7.2 toward the known width over a term's summits.

## 8. Where the player sees it

Interface builds; Content writes the words.
- **The person card:** five rows, each an icon, the skill's name, the range
  (e.g. 8–13) and its word. For the incarnated leader, the exact value.
- **The leader's sheet,** in the Presidency domain (`domains.gd`): the
  leader's own three skills (intrigue, persuasion, diplomacy), exact. The
  government's two (parliament, administration) are shown under the head of
  government's name, as that person's ranges, where they are not the leader
  (GLM's finding 5). Each has one line saying what it moves in the player's
  units, for example "Intrigue 11: the press and the courts find you 5 % less
  often".
- **Glossary terms** `skill_intrigue` ... `skill_administration`, marked in
  that prose ([[glossary-tooltips]]).
- No new verb. Skills are read, not spent.

## 9. Bands (declared before any build)

| Band | Declared | Probe |
|---|---|---|
| **K1, every skill matters somewhere** (each alone at 15 against 10) | intrigue: S1's pooled exposure falls at least 10 % relative; persuasion: L1's mean picked odds rise at least 0.03; diplomacy: relations gained per act rise at least 10 %; parliament: the governing coalition's rebels in an unpopular-government division fall at least 10 %; administration: mean effectiveness rises at least 0.03 | `shadows_probe`, `lever_probe`, `diplomacy_probe`, `balance_probe`, `cabinet_probe` |
| **K2, no skill decides everything** (each skill alone at 0 and at 20, on the office that carries it: the leader for three, the head of government for two) | B5's rows (honest each ≥ 5 %, none > 40 %, 0 dominated pairs, the shadow rows) pass; B6's once band stays in **[5 %, 45 %]**; B8's catch rows pass by the gap rule; the played party's vote share in every `balance_probe` regime moves by at most **3 points** against all-10 | the same probes |
| **K3, the derived spread** | per skill over the 1,848 derived: mean in [9, 11], standard deviation in [2, 3.5], none outside [3, 17] | a count over `data/` |
| **K3b, the authored spread** | per skill over the 301 met cast: mean in [9, 13] (the migrated intrigue alone already reads about 12.0: 20 × the authored mean 0.60, GLM's finding 13); bounded people in [6, 16] | `tools/check_skills.py` |
| **K4, the fog** | width 7.2 for a stranger, 1.6 known; the truth inside 100 %; each window inside the last at a fixed truth | a `lever_probe` L1-style read |
| **K5, the migration changes nothing** | with `intrigue` = 20 × `social_skill` and no new read switched on, every balance, audience, lever and shadows table matches its predecessor to 1e-12, with no row's verdict changed | the four probes |

- **Why K5 is not `cmp`:** 13 of the 47 distinct authored values do not
  survive one-decimal × 20 and ÷ 20 in binary floats; 0.34 comes back one
  unit off (MEASURED over `data/people`). A byte-identical claim would fail
  for a reason that changes nothing (GLM's finding 9).
- **K2's extremes are stress tests.** No played leader sits at 0 or 20 today
  (the heads read 8.4 to 16.0). The bands bound what the content may ever
  author, not what this build plays (GLM's finding 6, read as intended).
- **K1 and K2 bracket each span.** If a span cannot meet K1's floor without
  breaking K2's ceiling, the author sees both numbers and the span shrinks
  (GLM's finding 15).
- **Why K2 is the discriminating band:** a skill that alone flips a band is a
  skill that decides the game. The 3 points are about a third of a typical
  regime's spread (OPINION).
- **The order of re-baselines (one each, measured, shown to the author).**
  The base is c1c's merged build, its turned rate settled (GLM's finding 14):
  1. the migration, identical (K5);
  2. the derived bench, which moves catch and lever odds for people who read
     0.5 today. France's people are all authored, so France's probes stay
     identical, and only foreign audiences move;
  3. the exposure multiplier;
  4. persuasion;
  5. knowledge speed;
  6. diplomacy, which moves the AI's diplomacy too;
  7. parliament;
  8. administration.

  Each is one declared run against its predecessor, under the stop rule.
- **Failure looks like:** a skill whose probe line does not move (decorative);
  a leader at 20 who wins every regime; a derived crowd of 17s; a range that
  jumps outside the last one.
