# Someday

> [!warning] Not part of near-term planning
> Nothing on this page is scheduled, estimated, or committed to. It exists so good ideas have somewhere to live that is not the [[ROADMAP]]. Treat it as a parking lot, not a backlog.

> [!note] Parked is not the same as declined
> Everything here is *unscheduled*. Things that were considered and **decided
> against**, with a reason and a route back, are in [[declined]] instead.

## Governance

- Gerrymandering and boundary review
- By-elections and mid-term seat changes
- Turnout modelling as a force distinct from opposition
- Franchise and voting-age changes
- Filibuster and procedural obstruction
- Committee system routing and burying bills
- Private members' bills — the assembly proposing laws *at* the player
- Vote buying, pork-barrel spending for individual votes
- Emergency powers, and their corrosive effect on legitimacy
- Civil service quality as an execution layer independent of intent
- Intelligence service oversight
- Judicial appointments and slow court-packing
- Federalism and devolution
- Secession crises
- Impeachment and removal as a second lose condition
- Coup risk
- Monarchical succession and contested inheritance

## Economy

- Tax evasion and enforcement spending
- Named austerity and stimulus packages
- Sovereign wealth funds
- Sovereign default as a terminal debt-spiral state
- ~~Energy mix policy with emissions and dependency consequences~~ **Lifted to [[v2|V2.8]] and [[v2|V2.9]]**, and recorded as a lift rather than left to look as though it was always scheduled. Plants are ordered and **placed by click**, which is the first verb in this game where the player writes a coordinate.
- Housing market as its own crisis generator
- Automation and productivity versus employment
- ~~Regional economic divergence~~ **Lifted to [[v2|V2.5]] and [[v2|V2.6]]**. It stops being a mechanic to build once national GDP is the sum of regions: divergence is then what the model does rather than a thing added to it.
- ~~Corporate lobbying shifting policy and party positions~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.5]]** — a firm spends against a law it dislikes through the assembly arithmetic that already exists.
- ~~Autonomous corporate decision-making — firms investing, relocating, hiring and firing on their own~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.5]]** — reachable once regions ([[v2|V2.2]]) and the network ([[v3|V3.4]]) make a relocation mean something.
- ~~Foreign direct investment~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.5]]**, as the other half of a foreign rival being able to buy a firm.
- ~~Strategic asset protection against foreign takeover~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.5]]** — the player verb the takeover row creates the need for.
- ~~Bankruptcies and bailout decisions~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.5]]**, and it is a conversation rather than a line item because the firm has a chief ([[v4|V4.2]]).
- ~~State-owned enterprises with patronage dynamics~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.5]]**; the patronage half needs [[v4|V4.2]]'s people to mean anything.
- Central bank independence, interest rates, quantitative easing
- Currency and exchange rate — only meaningful once non-euro playable nations exist
- Capital controls

## Foreign policy

- Cultural and educational exchange as soft power
- Diaspora politics
- Export controls
- ~~Resource dependency and who holds the supply~~ **Lifted to [[v2|V2.7]] and [[v2|V2.8]]** — import dependency is what an energy mix is *for*, which is why that block reaches [[trade-model]] and not only the budget.
- Supply chain disruption propagating through trade links
- ~~Bloc accession and withdrawal as multi-year domestic fights~~ **Lifted to [[v3|V3.2]]** — the register made membership data, and the fight is referendum-first with an authored countdown ([[orgs-model]]).
- ~~UN-shaped international organisations, vetoes, resolutions~~ **Vetoes lifted to [[v4|V4.4]]** on the org register's own council machinery. The UN itself stays here.
- ~~Treaty ratification fights in your own assembly~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.4]]** — the strongest single link between this game's two halves, and it costs no new machine.
- War declaration and strategic-level resolution — units, theatres, casualties. The domestic-politics half of defence (capability, deployments, alliance obligations) moved to [[v1|V1.3]]; this half stays here, because it is a second game
- Arms sales
- Espionage, counter-intelligence, cyber operations
- Border disputes
- Nuclear deterrent posture
- Migration flows between nations
- Climate accords with binding domestic cost
- Pandemics and simultaneous global shocks
- Terrorism

## Society

- Regional and generational opinion breakdown
- State media ownership
- Social media dynamics and fast opinion swings
- Disinformation, foreign and domestic
- ~~Riots, police response choices, general strikes~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.2]]** as the collective half of the character layer, on Victoria 3's Passive → Agitating → Protesting → Rioting escalation, reading [[unrest-model]]'s grievance stock rather than a second meter.
- Revolution as an unrest terminal state and lose condition
- Personal corruption and exposure risk, including **the leader's personal wealth** as a tracked resource. Deliberately one line rather than two: a personal-money pool that is not corrupt is a second currency with no lose condition attached, and the version worth building is enrichment-with-exposure-risk, which needs the media and scandal layer of [[v1|V1.5]] to have any teeth
- ~~Patronage appointments~~ **Lifted to [[v4|V4.2]]** — an appointment is only patronage once the person appointed wants something.
- Separatist movements
- Ethnic and linguistic minority rights
- Religious institutions as independent authority
- Crime rate responding to policing, unemployment and inequality
