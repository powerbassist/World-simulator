# V3 — Europe, Whole (signed by the author 2026-09-06)

> [!note] Signed
> Drafted 2026-09-05 by a planning session, off a fresh playtest of current
> main ([[playtest-2026-09-05-b]] -- reports/, gitignored) and the V2.9b agency
> probe. The author signed the tier at the V3.1 plan gate on 2026-09-06. Every
> row here is still a hypothesis on [[v2]]'s own rule — each block gets a
> decomposition session that re-counts machines and surfaces before it builds.

The organising goal: **the Union the player governs inside becomes the game.**
V2 made the world visible and located; the playtest that closed it proved the
player's seat works and left two measured structural gaps — **the EU is one
panel with eight members and an empty council**, and **max zoom halves the
speed of the game itself**. V3 turns the map into a platform that can carry
Europe, generalizes the bloc into an alliance system, grows the roster to the
EU-27, and — first, because everything else is content on mush until it lands —
makes one term of play answer the player's hand.

## The playtest evidence this tier is built on

All from [[playtest-2026-09-05-b]] unless linked:

- **The seat works.** Opening card, answer-from-card, arrival pause, verb
  acknowledgment cards, and an election that owns the screen with attribution
  above the table — every V2.9 mechanism verified in live state, one full term
  to RE-ELECTION, zero script errors.
- **Agency still fails, now legibly.** The term's own attribution lines read
  "Public finances — helped (14%)" against "Income tax — hurt (10%)" and
  "Unemployment — hurt (21%)" — the V2.9b verdict (idle 26.4% beats active
  23.4%) rendered in the game's own voice.
- **The Union is 8 members, 2 authored proposals, one panel**, `rules: {}` in
  live state; `data/eu_parliament.json` carries 8 seats tables of a real
  27-member, 720-seat parliament. **3 of 10 nations are playable.**
- **Map render cost eats the clock**: 1.12 game-days/sec at globe view,
  0.50 at max zoom (speed 1, RTX 3070 Ti, 2560×1440) — with today's 10
  nations, 112 regions, 581 cities.
- **The network V2.7 modeled draws nothing** — no roads, no arcs, no convoys.

## V3.0 as built (2026-09-05, branch `v3.0-agency`)

The bands were pre-declared and every one judged (report:
`reports/agency-probe-v3.0.md`, gitignored). **The gap between the best
active style and idle closed −7.57 → −2.00 approval points and 3.0 → 0.2
vote points; the GATE's +1.0 approval clause is a MEASURED BOUND, not met.**
What shipped: honeymoon and fatigue as a **pure read of
`Elections.months_in_term`** (the S5 deferral needed no field and no save
move — the counter existed since V1.2); the fiscal re-weight (income scale
24, shared tax 16, spend 6; `DEFICIT_SCALE_PP` refused because halving it
clamps the spend side's pain); the grievance **relief** term (V2.9b F2's
path back, gain-amplified on the improving side); `UNREST_THRESHOLD_PCT`
re-banded 45 → 14 after the same scales took the austerity peak to 18.02
and left the strike agent unreachable; B4 (campaign power) **REFUSED** at
the measured +0.4-vote ceiling; B5 (outcome ordering) monotone, no swing
change. The re-run's outcome flips all map to mechanisms: hands_off,
redistributive and managed_then_redistributive **lose their 2032
re-elections to fatigue** (the playtest's F6 ask working), and
managed_captures_the_courts **gains** its 2027 re-election to the honeymoon
— a side effect flagged, not tuned away. The FAIL's design meaning and the
three routes past it are the probe report's last section — **the author
holds that call**; this session deliberately took none.

## V3.1 as built (2026-09-06, on `main`)

**The step-0 profile refuted the row's premise in both halves and kept its warning.**
The committed instrument (`tools/frame_probe.gd`) measured the clock HEALTHY under P4's
own regime — 0.998–0.999 days/sec at max zoom, vsync on, 1440p, pause state recorded —
because `clock.gd` is time-based with catch-up and render cost cannot slow it; P4's
0.50 days/sec was 4/8, a clock pause (the V2.9 decision-arrival pause is the likeliest
biter) inside a sample whose pause state was not recorded. And there is no deep-zoom
cliff: true frame cost ran 1.02–1.07× globe view across the whole zoom range before any
work. What was real: a **~3.6 ms zoom-independent constant** — ~900 draw calls and 68k
primitives replayed every frame. So the block shipped the thing the measurement
justified, not the LOD platform the row sketched: **off-screen culling inside each pass**
(lon/lat bbox per ring, derived 8 px pad, two-pass enclave rule preserved by building the
hairline list from culled-through rings; city glyphs culled on screen points because
their populations are live), the **minimap redraw on view change**, and the gate restated
on frame time — **max zoom now 0.51× globe view (was 1.07×), map cost at deep zoom
1.33 ms (declared: ≤ 2.0 ms)**, deep zoom viewport-bounded and therefore EU-27-proof by
construction, everything-visible band projected ~7–8 ms at 1440p with ~2× headroom.
Refusals with their reasons in [docs/map-platform-model.md](docs/map-platform-model.md):
per-tile raster LOD (the raster is one quad), glyph clustering (no overlap at current
thresholds), cached fills (the engine already caches), polyline LOD (the named-but-
refused lever if a V3.3 tranche breaks the everything-visible budget). Identity held at
**0 differing bytes × 4 zooms**, same-tree, clock frozen. `git diff main..HEAD --
src/sim/ src/core/` empty, `SAVE_VERSION` untouched, no balance re-run — U-track only,
sixth session running.

## V3.2 as built (2026-09-06, branch `v3.2-alliance`)

**The step-0 audit corrected the handoff on one load-bearing point before any
code:** `bloc_offset` is the VOTER-bloc opinion channel (six electoral blocs)
and carries nothing org-shaped; the bloc machine's opinion surface is the
`europe` issue, one-signed by design (bloc-model §1). So "multiple orgs writing
the same channels" resolved to ONE clamped burden — no second approval number
is structurally possible because no second channel is touched. The audit also
found membership DUAL-SOURCED (nation files' `government.eu` blocks plus
`data/eu_parliament.json`, held consistent only by cross-validation); the
register collapsed it to one source and the cross-validation died with it.

**Track (a) — the register — is bit-identical, as predicted: 1680 of 1680
printed lines, zero outcome changes.** `src/sim/orgs.gd` replaces `bloc.gd`;
`src/core/org.gd` is the content type; `data/orgs/*.json` holds the four
authored orgs (eu, eurozone, schengen, nato); `NationState.orgs` is LIVE
membership; `SAVE_VERSION` 32 on the bloc→orgs rename (three fields, the
bloc-model argument verbatim) plus `GameState.accessions` serialised from day
one. The two co-member prices moved into eu.json UNCHANGED (relations 15.0,
trade 0.50) and the eu agenda preserved flat-file order — that is what the
identity run proves. `Orgs.init_state()` runs BEFORE `Diplomacy.seed_relations()`
because resting relations now read live membership.

**Track (b) — the fights and the content — moved exactly through its named
channels.** Accession/withdrawal is referendum-first (machinery reused whole),
then an authored negotiation countdown, then the flip; the first draft's
withdrawal position measured yes_share 0.347 — unwinnable — and the test caught
it, so the shipped positions are swept on Referendum's own curve (0.510–0.523
at rest). The 2 % floor MOVED to NATO's new council (authored, population-shaped
seat weights — NATO has none in reality and the machinery needs numbers),
taking the eu agenda 7 → 6. Embassies and foreign aid landed on the relations
channel, the two parked V1 rows. Measured: 47 of 51 regimes differ at 3–5
last-digit lines; the ONE outcome change of 99 outcome lines is GBR
`managed_abolishes_the_monarchy` — a 2029 rounding-boundary wobble compounding
through the openness channel (trend 1.32 → 1.30, V1.3f's own signature) into a
2039 election flip. Suite 80/80; screenshot frames judged in-session; the pass
found one BUG and it was in the tool (a rename the staging hadn't caught),
visible only because the frame's own numbers disagreed. Refusals with reasons in
[docs/orgs-model.md](docs/orgs-model.md): no org-side consent, no AI desire for
membership, Eurozone and Schengen zero-priced with a named trigger, no war.

## The calls this draft makes, all challengeable

**Agency before content.** The probe's numbers are the band input: the
`public_finances` credit is real (+0.75 satisfaction units for a 4.5-point
deficit improvement) and out-muscled by tax pain (−0.5 per 4 points of income
tax); grievance explodes ~15 points for ANY fiscal change because `hardship()`
re-reads the same satisfaction pool Opinion does; campaign cannot touch
approval by construction; a 0.3-point fragmentation flip decides elections.
V3.0 is the tuning session the probe handed off, plus **honeymoon and
fatigue** — the oldest open deferral on the project (S5), an opinion-model
mechanism, and this is the opinion session. The two cheap standing V1 rows
(**embassies, foreign aid**) ride in V3.2's content pass rather than owning a
session.

**The map is a platform before it is a picture.** The measured gate: at EU-27
glyph counts, **max zoom must run within ~10% of globe-view speed** (today it
runs at 45%). Likely work, to be settled by a frame-cost profile before any
of it is built: zoom-banded vector detail (the 1:110m/1:50m/1:10m sets are
already committed), city glyph culling/clustering off-screen and at band
edges, cached/batched region fills, and the raster's role at deep zoom
(re-projected per-tile LOD is the expensive option; a vector-first deep zoom
is the cheap one). Roster expansion triples the city and region counts —
**the platform pass is before V3.3 on purpose.** *(As built at V3.1, on the
gate's own terms: the profile settled the work before any of it was built,
and settled it smaller — see the as-built section above.)*

**The alliance system generalizes what exists rather than adding a parallel
one.** Today: `GameState.bloc` is ONE hardcoded object binding eight nations;
`GameState.pacts` is bilateral pair state (V1.3f's defensive pacts);
agreements and measures are pair channels. The move is a **multi-lateral org
register** — `data/orgs/*.json`: the EU (council, parliament, rules,
transfers — everything `bloc.gd` does today, minus the hardcoding), the
Eurozone, Schengen, NATO, and authored custom alliances — with membership as
data, shared decision machinery (the council vote V1.3d built, pointed at N
orgs), and **accession and withdrawal as multi-year domestic fights**
([[someday]]'s own row, finally reachable once membership is data). Bilateral
pacts and the org register stay distinct shapes on the V1.3a rule: a pact is
pair state, an org binds N nations at once. EU institutions (commission
agenda, parliament groups) are CONTENT on this machine, sized by the
decomposition session.

**The roster grows in tranches, and the register leads.** +19 nations to the
EU-27 (BGR HRV CYP CZE DNK EST FIN GRC HUN IRL LTU LVA MLT POL PRT ROU SVK
SVN SWE; GBR and CHE stay as the non-EU neighbors they already are).
Everything a nation carries today must arrive with each tranche: nation file,
parties, political system, people (portraits via the committed V2.3 recipe,
~7 more per nation), regions and cities through the V2.2 import (Eurostat
NUTS covers all 27; MLT and CYP are single-region nations on [[v2|V2.5]]'s
Luxembourg precedent, and islands reach the graph through authored sea links
on V2.7's own rule). The natural cut is **(a) register + AI nations** —
import, geometry, economy, no politics — then **(b) political depth +
playable + portraits**. CITY_MIN_POP is re-swept, not inherited: ~1500-1800
named cities against 581, on the drawing-side clause V2.2a wrote. The balance
harness re-baselines; bit-identity is impossible across a roster change and
the prediction to write down first is WHICH existing runs move and why.

**Roads draw the graph the sim already prices.** V2.7's adjacency + eight sea
links + accessibility are live and invisible. The cheap, licence-clean shape:
**generated spines between adjacent regions' principal cities** (the geometry
is derived from committed data — the ODbL refusal that killed routed geometry
never arises), zoom-gated like the city glyphs, with sea links drawn as
routes. Natural Earth 1:10m roads (public domain, but sparse in Europe) are
the fallback the step-0 probe decides between. This block carries V2.11's
arcs and convoys on top: trade arcs zoomed out, ships and lorries on the
spines zoomed in — motion the platform pass (V3.1) paid for.

*(As built at V3.4, branch `v3.4-roads-and-motion`: **the track was written
`S + U` and the block built `U`.** The block draws the graph the sim already
prices and `src/sim/` never learns it exists —
`git diff main..HEAD --stat -- src/sim/ src/core/` produces no output, and
`SAVE_VERSION` stayed at 33 with no tick call added and no balance regime
created. The re-run was predicted bit-identical BEFORE it was run, and was —
the figures are in the as-built row in AGENTS.md. The `S` half of the written
track was never spent: not one simulation number moved. A future reader can
check the claim the same way, by running that diff against `main`.)*

**Climate rides the org system.** Emissions are an output of V2.8's mix;
sited disasters are the event schema with a place; **climate accords are
V3.2's council machinery pointed at a cost the player's own industry pays**
— the shape [[v2|V2.10]] named, now with 27 members and a real accession
fight around it.

**The front door and the ceremony.** v2.md §9 parked sound and tally
animation here explicitly ("V3, unless the screenshot pass argues otherwise";
it didn't get argued out of existence by one). This block was drafted
LAST and is small: menu presence (the 2026-09-05 playtest's "menu shows none of
the game"), election sting and tally, and whatever the agency session proves
the player needs to be TOLD rather than shown. *(No longer the tier's last
block: V3.7 was appended after it on 2026-09-07, and the V3 close-out moves
with it.)*

**The portrait wall is rebuilt, and two V2.3 premises are retired.** Added
2026-09-07 at the author's call, after the front door. V2.3 shipped 142 duotone
busts and answered "background per situation" with a **nation-hue band**
(AGENTS.md:507, display-side, one canonical PNG); the author now wants the real
thing. **The duotone dies and realism replaces it**, the band is dropped
entirely, and a face gains five expressions (`neutral`, `pleased`, `stern`,
`worried`, `weary`) on a transparent cut-out the game composites over **42
situational backdrops** at the moment of display — which is what finally makes
`portrait_card.gd`'s mood emoji redundant. The two gaps V2.3 left are the
reason it is worth a block: the prompt carries no region, no role and no
wardrobe — a Finnish and a Greek minister are drawn from one distribution — and
its `GENDER` first-name table defaults unknown names to male, which across 213
people in 27 nations is most of them. Art direction stays OUT of the register:
age, sex and wardrobe live in a generator-side sidecar, because
`src/core/person.gd` is id-and-name by construction and role is already stated
by the slot pointing at the person. Measured on the author's machine, not
assumed: **8 GB VRAM** binds the method to Impact Pack's `FaceDetailer` inpaint
rather than Flux Kontext (11 GB) or Qwen-Image-Edit, and the committed wall is
**~59 MB as WebP q90 at 432×592 against ~345 MB as PNG** — regeneration churn,
not the first wall, is what decides that. **Split in two on the author's
instruction: the build session fires no GPU.** It ships the eleven machines
behind a legacy PNG path that holds the repo green and playable; the ~2.5 h
generation run and the asset commit are a separate, later phase. **The V3
close-out lands here rather than at V3.6.**

## What V3 deliberately does not open

- **War** stays in [[someday]] where V1.3g left it — an alliance system is
  not a war system, and NATO here is Article-5-as-domestic-politics (V1.3f's
  own cut), not theatres.
- **Global scope / ~195 nations** stays parked; the EU-27 + 2 neighbors is
  the roster this game is for.
- **Non-European playable nations** stay parked with it.
- **A second simulation loop** (provinces-as-nations, sub-national play):
  the roster grows, the game does not fork.
